Fazer Chocolate Factory

Founded in 1891 by Karl Fazer, Fazer is one of Finland's best known brands and a genuine piece of everyday Finnish culture — the blue Fazer Sininen milk chocolate bar is a fixture in almost every Finnish kitchen cupboard. The factory and visitor centre in Vantaa, just outside Helsinki, offers a close-up look at how the chocolate is made, from roasting the cocoa beans to tempering and moulding the finished bars. It's a place where tradition meets modernity, offering visitors a unique glimpse into the world of Finnish chocolate making.
